OPTIONAL EVIDENCE REVIEW

TP53 S127: hypothesis-generating only

The site label is not proof of biological activity. This review keeps TP53 separate from similarly named Ser127 results reported for other proteins.

Current interpretation

The previous six-gene project produced a site-level sensitivity pattern labelled TP53 S127, but the protein-level primary analysis was negative and the independent BRCA-METABRIC / LUAD-OncoSG analyses did not reproduce a significant protein-level TP53 proximity signal. Therefore S127 is not a validated discovery, is not a novel PTM-site claim, and is not a replicated cancer mechanism.

A new cohort is displayed here only through the real mapped site-level output. The website does not hard-code p-values or a “significant” status for S127.

Sources checked

  • Reviewed human TP53 UniProtKB entry (P04637)
  • Reproducible PubMed query: TP53 Ser127 phosphorylation
  • Review: regulation of p53 by phosphorylation
  • A frequent “Ser127” search result concerns YAP1—not TP53

What is not established

The targeted search checked on 9 October 2026 did not establish a specific independent functional validation for human TP53 pSer127. This is not proof that no such study exists; it was not a systematic review. Search hits for YAP1 Ser127 must not be attributed to TP53.

Evidence threshold: direct site-specific evidence plus a pre-specified primary-level result that survives independent same-cancer validation. A permissive distance cutoff or isolated site-level p-value is insufficient.
