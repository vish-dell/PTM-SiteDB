Existing work and the defensible research gap

PTM–mutation spatial co-clustering is established prior work. The project therefore makes a reproducibility / hypothesis-testing contribution, not a first-of-kind claim.

Contribution statement

“This project implements a reproducible hypothesis-testing workflow that treats experimentally recorded PTM residues as explicit structural anchors, maps mutations and sites onto a selected protein sequence/structure, tests proximity under a gene-specific trinucleotide-context-matched coding-opportunity null, benchmarks gene-level results against an external driver catalog, and exposes QC failures and independent replication outcomes.”

Do not claim: “No prior tool integrates PTMs and 3D cancer mutations,” “first-of-kind,” or “validated S127 discovery.”
