01 · Scale the evidence

Bootstrap the official dbPTM experimental phosphorylation table and the official IntOGen driver archive; the archive-declared release is recorded in the source manifest. The panel is based on observed source intersections and reports when the 200-gene target was not reached.

02 · Match mutation opportunity

Enumerate all missense single-nucleotide opportunities for one selected MANE/Ensembl-canonical transcript per gene. The null preserves each gene's observed SBS96 context counts.

03 · Keep the inference honest

Missing structures, transcript mismatches and absent context opportunities become explicit QC statuses. They are not treated as negative biological results.
