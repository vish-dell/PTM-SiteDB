# TP53 Ser127 evidence review

**Review checked:** 9 October 2026.

## What can be said

The previous small-panel analysis reported a site-level sensitivity pattern around the residue labelled TP53 S127, but its protein-level primary analysis was negative and independent same-cancer validation did not reproduce a significant protein-level TP53 signal. The expanded website does not hard-code S127; it displays a site only if the current experimental PTM input, sequence mapping, structure mapping and actual cohort result all contain that site.

A targeted PubMed search was carried out for TP53/Ser127/phosphorylation and for PTM plus 3D mutation clustering. The results reviewed here did **not establish a specific, independent functional validation of human TP53 pSer127**. This is not proof that no such paper exists: the search was targeted, not a systematic review. A key search pitfall is that many results about “Ser127 phosphorylation” refer to **YAP1**, not TP53; these proteins must not be conflated.

- Reviewed human TP53 UniProtKB entry (P04637): https://www.uniprot.org/uniprot/P04637
- Reproducible PubMed query: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=%22TP53%22+AND+%22Ser127%22+AND+phosphorylation
- A commonly returned but different-protein result concerns YAP Ser127: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20729916/
- General review of p53 phosphorylation regulation: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20157532/

## Interpretation rule

Treat TP53 S127 as a **hypothesis-generating database/site candidate only**. Do not call it a novel PTM site, a functional cancer mechanism, a replicated signal, or a validated biomarker without direct site-specific evidence and an independently replicated primary-level result. A broader or permissive 3D threshold does not substitute for replication.
