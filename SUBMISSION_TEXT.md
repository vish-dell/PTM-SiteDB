## Results
The production exact test was validated by simulation: no null scenario exceeded nominal alpha, power was adequate only for strong effects (RR >= 3 with >= 20 mutated positions), and the false-positive rate rose to 0.12-0.14 under regional mutation-rate variation. After a duplicated-input problem was detected and corrected, and the original benchmark panel was restored, TCGA PAAD (176 samples) and PRAD (493 samples) were analysed with no sample overlap, against the hg19 (GRCh37) reference. The pre-specified primary endpoint (decoy-calibrated, burden-stratified test of driver vs not-listed genes) was estimable in both cohorts (98 and 99 genes tested; 43 not-listed genes with a decoy score in each) and was null (one-sided p = 0.796 in PAAD and 0.555 in PRAD). Replication was therefore not claimed. The burden-stratified raw-score test was also null (p = 0.629 and 0.264), and no gene reached q < 0.05 in any of 12 threshold settings per cohort. An uncorrected secondary Mann-Whitney comparison in PRAD gave p = 0.012; it is not burden-adjusted and is not interpreted as a finding. In a single-gene screen of brca_tcga and brca_metabric (TP53, AKT1, CTNNB1; 5000 permutations, seed 1), TP53 reached protein-level q < 0.05 in brca_tcga at 20 A only, not at 10 or 50 A and not in brca_metabric. This is reported as exploratory and unreplicated.

## Calibration of the tests
In simulation, the naive and SBS96 tests had false-positive rates of 2.4-6.0% (nominal 5%), whereas the decoy-anchor test rejected at 6.5-13.5%, so decoy-calibrated p-values are treated as exploratory. Under a PTM-specific effect the decoy test had higher power (for example 67% vs 49% for SBS96 at RR 3 with 20 mutations), but this is not a like-for-like gain because its size was inflated.

## Limitations
The database holds 30 phosphosites; low-confidence AlphaFold regions exclude 11 of 15 TP53 sites and both EGFR sites. Kinase, disease and evidence annotations are not independently verified, and some PubMed links are general rather than site-specific. Distances use AlphaFold C-alpha coordinates only. "Not listed" does not mean non-driver. The exact test is not robust to regional mutation-rate variation. The decoy control was not applied to the single-gene web screen. Only about 160-170 missense variants per cohort fall in the panel, so a null result at these counts is weak evidence of absence.
